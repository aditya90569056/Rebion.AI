Laboratory Skill Development Camp

REBION AI • Events • 4 min read

Great science begins at the bench. Our Laboratory Skill Development Camp is a hands-on event designed to help students master the practical techniques that form the foundation of biotechnology and life-science research.

Learn by doing — real instruments, real protocols and real laboratory confidence.

Skills You Will Build

  • Safe handling of laboratory equipment and reagents
  • Core techniques in molecular biology and microbiology
  • Accurate measurement, pipetting and sample preparation
  • Maintaining a scientific notebook and recording results
  • Basic troubleshooting and good laboratory practice

Why Hands-On Skills Matter

Theory alone is never enough in the life sciences. Whether you are preparing for research, higher studies or an industry role, the ability to confidently perform standard laboratory procedures sets you apart and prepares you for real environments.

Camp Format

The camp combines short demonstrations with extensive hands-on practice, guided by experienced instructors. Participants work in small groups to ensure personal attention and plenty of practice time.

Who Should Join

Undergraduate and postgraduate students of biotechnology, microbiology, biochemistry and allied fields, as well as faculty and technicians who want to refresh their practical skills.

Registration

Seats are limited. Contact our team for camp dates, venue and registration details.
