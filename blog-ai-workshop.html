AI in Biotechnology Workshop 2026

REBION AI • Workshop Desk • 4 min read

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way scientists explore biology. From predicting protein structures to accelerating drug discovery, AI is now at the heart of modern biotechnology. Our AI in Biotechnology Workshop 2026 is designed to give students and researchers a practical, hands-on introduction to this exciting intersection.

A two-day, hands-on workshop that connects machine learning fundamentals with real biological datasets and problems.

What You Will Learn

  • Fundamentals of AI and machine learning for the life sciences
  • Working with biological and genomic datasets using Python
  • Sequence analysis, classification and prediction models
  • Introduction to tools used in bioinformatics and computational biology
  • Real-world case studies and guided mini-projects

Who Should Attend

The workshop is ideal for undergraduate and postgraduate students of biotechnology, microbiology, genetics, pharmacy and computer science, as well as faculty members and research scholars who want to add AI skills to their scientific toolkit. No prior programming experience is required.

Why It Matters

Biotechnology is generating more data than ever before. The professionals who can combine biological insight with AI and data skills will lead the next wave of discovery. This workshop is a stepping stone toward that future and is aligned with REBION AI's mission of building AI for tomorrow's leaders.

How to Register

Seats are limited and allotted on a first-come, first-served basis. Reach out to our team through the contact page to confirm dates, fees and your seat.
